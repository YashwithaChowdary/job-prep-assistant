## Projects

**AI-Powered Job Preparation Assistant** | Python, Streamlit, Claude API, pytest
- Built a resume-to-job matching tool (txt/docx/pdf in) that reports skill gaps, a prioritised learning plan and interview questions.
- Designed rules-first extraction with an optional LLM layer whose output is validated against facts, so it cannot invent skills or gaps.
- Hand-labelled 9 resume/job pairs; ablation study showed alias handling worth 15 points of gap accuracy; stress cases exposed and fixed 5 bugs.
- Shipped CLI, Streamlit app, 14 automated tests.
